## Overview
Kamptal lies in Niederösterreich (Lower Austria), around the town of Langenlois and the Kamp river, about an hour from Vienna. Together with neighbouring Kremstal and the Wachau, it is a heartland of Austrian dry Grüner Veltliner and Riesling. Kamptal DAC was established with the 2008 vintage.

## Typical grapes
Grüner Veltliner and Riesling are the backbone of Kamptal DAC. Grüner brings white pepper and citrus, while Riesling is precise and mineral. From the 2024 vintage Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc and Pinot Gris may also be sold as Kamptal DAC. Red varieties such as Zweigelt and Pinot Noir are grown too, but are sold without DAC status.

## Wine styles
Kamptal DAC follows an origin pyramid: regional wine (Gebietswein), village wine (Ortswein) from one of twelve named wine villages, and single-vineyard wine (Riedenwein) from a classified Ried. The term Reserve marks riper, richer wines released later. Many top vineyards are also classified as Erste Lage by the Österreichische Traditionsweingüter growers' association.

## What makes it special
The great Heiligenstein vineyard near Zöbing, a steep slope of Permian desert sandstone with volcanic elements, produces some of Austria's most celebrated Rieslings. Elsewhere, loess and primary rock soils provide variety, and Gaisberg and Lamm are also renowned sites. Cool air from the Waldviertel plateau meets warm Pannonian air from the east, giving big day-night temperature swings that preserve aroma and acidity. From the 2025 vintage most Kamptal DAC wine must come from certified organic or sustainable vineyards.
