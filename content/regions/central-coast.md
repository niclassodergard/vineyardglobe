## Overview
The Central Coast is a very large California region running along the Pacific from the San Francisco Bay Area south to Santa Barbara County. It spans many AVAs with very different climates, from cool coastal valleys to hot inland basins.

## Typical grapes
Paso Robles is warm and favours Zinfandel, Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah and Rhône blends with Grenache. Santa Barbara and Edna Valley are cooler and suit Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Syrah.

## Wine styles
Paso Robles reds are ripe, rich and spicy, with Rhône-style blends a regional strength. Santa Barbara gives more restrained, fresher Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and cool-climate Syrah.

## What makes it special
Unusual east-west valleys in Santa Barbara County, such as the Santa Ynez Valley, funnel cool Pacific air inland, giving long, slow ripening; the Sta. Rita Hills AVA is especially noted for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Paso Robles has large day-night temperature swings and calcareous soils in its western hills. The region is known for experimentation and diversity of styles, helped by less rigid traditions than older regions.
