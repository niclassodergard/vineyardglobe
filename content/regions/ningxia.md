## Overview
Ningxia is a Hui autonomous region in northwest China, and the Helan Mountain East area, between the Helan Mountains and the Yellow River at roughly 1,100 metres, is its main wine zone. It is among China's most prominent regions for quality wine, and the government has encouraged its development.

## Typical grapes
Bordeaux varieties dominate, led by Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, with some Cabernet Franc. Cabernet Gernischt, long grown in China and since identified as Carmenère, is also planted, and Marselan is increasingly promoted as a Chinese speciality. Chardonnay is the main white.

## Wine styles
Dry red wines in a Bordeaux style are the focus, typically with ripe dark fruit, moderate to firm tannin and fresh acidity. Some producers use French oak ageing. White and sparkling wines are made in smaller amounts.

## What makes it special
The Helan Mountains shelter the vineyards from cold winds and desert sand from the northwest, and the Yellow River provides irrigation in a desert-edge climate with sunny days and large day-night temperature swings. Winters are severe, so vines must be buried under soil each autumn and dug up in spring to survive, which adds labour and cost. Soils are mostly gravelly and sandy. The region has its own classification of wineries and an emphasis on estate-style chateaux, and it gained international attention when Helan Qingxue's Jia Bei Lan 2009 won a top trophy at the Decanter World Wine Awards in 2011.
